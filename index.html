Raven Wing

A personal desktop email client for Windows, made by its developer for personal use.

Raven Wing shows Gmail in a fast desktop window with near real-time notifications, folders, rules and a calendar built from meeting invitations. It runs entirely on the user's own computer: there is no Raven Wing server and no cloud service.

  • Reads mail and sends mail only. It cannot change or delete anything in Gmail.
  • Mail is stored only on the user's computer, in an encrypted database.
  • Nothing is collected, shared, sold or sent anywhere else.
